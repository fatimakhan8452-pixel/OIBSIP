THE JOURNEY

A Remarkable Life

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, remembered for his contributions to science, education and public service.

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest family, he developed a strong interest in learning, science and mathematics during his early years. His journey showed how determination, discipline and education can help a person overcome difficult circumstances.

Kalam became a distinguished aerospace scientist and played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. He worked with organisations including the Defence Research and Development Organisation and the Indian Space Research Organisation, contributing to major national scientific projects.

He became widely respected for his scientific leadership and his ability to connect with young people. As the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007, he was known for his approachable personality and his strong belief that students and young citizens could help shape the country's future.

Beyond science and public service, Kalam was an author, teacher and passionate advocate for education. He encouraged young people to dream ambitiously, develop knowledge and turn ideas into meaningful action. His words and work continue to inspire students across India.
